# Repeated self-promotion, harassment, and toxicity from Theta

This needs to be addressed because the situation with **Theta ([@ThetaHalo](https://github.com/ThetaHalo))** has gone well beyond simply promoting their own project.

Theta is the administrator and developer behind the mod in question, and the same person is responsible for the repeated self-promotion, behavior towards other developers, and interactions with me in the AOU Discord server.

There is nothing wrong with developing a mod, promoting it, or being proud of your work. The problem is when the promotion becomes constant while the project itself receives little meaningful development or maintenance, and that promotion is accompanied by harassment, trolling, and hostility towards people who use or develop other mods.

The contrast between the amount of promotion and the actual state of the project is difficult to ignore.

A mod should speak for itself through its code, functionality, maintenance, updates, and support. Repeatedly talking about a project does not make it active. Advertising it does not make it functional. And being an administrator or moderator in the AOU Discord does not give anyone a free pass to harass or antagonize other members.

## Theta's behavior

The issue is not simply that Theta promotes their mod.

The issue is the overall pattern of behavior surrounding it:

- Repeatedly self-promoting the mod in the AOU Discord
- Flaunting the project despite its lack of meaningful updates
- Trolling and provoking other developers
- Repeatedly antagonizing me in AOU quarantine chat when I wanted to talk to the boss
- Making discussions unnecessarily hostile and personal
- Harassing people who do not use or support the mod
- Attempting to undermine other mods and their developers
- Turning disagreements about mods into personal arguments
- Continuing to promote the project instead of addressing the problems and lack of maintenance

I have personally experienced repeated trolling, antagonizing, and toxic behavior from Theta in AOU chats.

This is not about simply disagreeing with Theta or disliking their project. Developers are allowed to criticize other projects and defend their own.

The problem is when that disagreement turns into repeated personal targeting and attempts to provoke others.

## The AOU Discord

This is particularly concerning because Theta is also involved in moderation and administration within the AOU Discord community.

A person with moderation responsibilities should not be using that position to contribute to hostility towards specific members or to repeatedly promote their own project.

The AOU Discord should be a place where different projects and developers can coexist without people being harassed simply because they chose a different mod.

Nobody should have to deal with repeated trolling or hostility simply because they develop or use a competing project.

## The state of the project

If the mod is functional and actively maintained, then the response to criticism should be simple:

- Fix the issues
- Release updates
- Respond to bug reports
- Improve documentation
- Demonstrate why users should use it

What does not help is constantly promoting the project while attacking or provoking people who point out its problems and ask for updates.

Projects become inactive. Developers lose interest. Updates stop. That is completely normal in open-source development.

There is nothing embarrassing about a project becoming inactive.

What is problematic is continuing to aggressively promote an inactive or unreliable project while simultaneously attacking people who have moved on to other projects.

Nobody owes a developer attention, users, praise, or adoption.

If Theta wants people to use the project, the best way to achieve that is through actual development, maintenance, reliability, and contributions.

Let the project speak for itself.

If someone wants users to choose their project, give them a reason to choose it through development and reliability.

Not harassment.

Not trolling.

Not never ending self-promotion.

The code and the work should speak for themselves.
